Tools I Built

I edit for a living. I also build the tools that make client work faster: one for revision notes, one for finding footage. Both are live. Try them below with sample footage.

Review

01

Revision notes without the email chains.

Clients used to send notes like “cut at 01:02” by email: one timestamp, no end point, easy to misread. Now they open one link, watch the edit, and leave notes right on the timeline.

  • scheduleTimed notes with a start and an end, plus replies
  • subtitlesSynced transcript, made automatically (English and Indonesian). Strike through words to cut them.
  • compareSame link for every version, with notes carried over to V2
  • smartphoneWorks on a phone, no account needed

B-Roll Library

02

Find the right shot in seconds.

Brand clients send hundreds of clips that end up scattered across drives. Each new clip is now described and tagged by AI, so editors search by meaning instead of scrubbing through folders.

  • searchSearch by meaning: “someone eating outdoors” finds it even if no file is named that
  • movieHover to scrub through a clip without opening it
  • folder_sharedA separate library per client, with similar-shot suggestions
  • downloadDownload one clip or a whole selection as a zip
